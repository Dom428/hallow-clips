# Hallow Clips roadmap

## Done

| Version | What it added |
|---|---|
| **1.0** | Instant replay: Ctrl+Alt+S saves the last 30 s (or your length) as an MP4; records only while a game runs; encoded on the graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Windows); game sound and your mic on separate tracks; clip list with thumbnails; FFmpeg set up with one click and checked |

## Next

### 1.1 - Fixes from real use
- Ctrl+Alt+S during a real game, and recording starting and stopping with games
- Check sound stays in step with the picture over long sessions

### 1.2 - Trim and share
- Trim a clip (start and end) without re-encoding
- Copy for Discord: a smaller version under Discord's upload size
- Rename clips and mark favourites

### 1.3 - More ways to record
- Record a whole session (start / stop), not just the replay
- HEVC and AV1 for smaller files on cards that support them
- Space limit for the clips folder, oldest non-favourites go to the Recycle Bin (off by default)

### 1.4 - Hallow family
- Save clip from Hallow Remote; "Clip saved" in Hallow Overlay
- This game's clips on its Hallow Hub page
- Opt-in Discord post with the clip through Hallow Toolkit's link (the webhook stays on this PC)
